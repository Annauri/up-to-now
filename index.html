🔔 Golden rule: if you say or ask WHEN, use the Past Simple.

Word order with adverbs

Adverbs have favourite places. Watch them drop into the sentence.

A. Between have/has and the verb

have / hasalready · just · ever · never · recentlyV3

She has already left. · We've just arrived. · I've never tried it.

B. At the end

… sentence …yet · lately · recently · so far · for… · since…

I haven't decided yet. · I've been sleeping badly lately.

C. Before haven't / hasn't

subjectstillhaven't / hasn'tV3

He still hasn't called me.

Questions

(Wh-)have / hassubjectever / already / justV3 / been V-ing…yet / lately?

Have you ever been to London? · Has the bus arrived yet? · Have you been sleeping well lately?

⚖️ already — positive sentences (in a question it shows surprise: Have you already finished?! That was fast!).
yet — negatives and questions. still + negative = something is later than expected.

Simple or Continuous?

Result or activity? How many or how long?

✅I've painted the kitchen.The job is finished. Look at the result!
🎨I've been painting the kitchen.Focus on the activity. Maybe not finished — that's why I'm covered in paint.
🔢I've written three emails.How many? A completed amount.
⏳I've been writing emails all morning.How long? The duration.
🤝 With live, work, study, teach + for/since, both are fine: I've lived / I've been living here for ten years. The continuous can sound more temporary.

Present Perfect or Past Simple?

Connected to now, or finished and gone?

🔑❌I've lost my keys.They are still lost now.
🔑✅I lost my keys yesterday, but I found them.Finished story at a finished time.
📆I've seen two films this week.The week isn't over.
🗓️I saw two films last week.Last week is finished.

In conversation we often start with the Present Perfect and give details in the Past Simple:

Have you ever been to Italy? 🇮🇹
Yes, I have! I went there in 2023. We stayed in Rome.
Did you like it?
🇬🇧 This lesson uses British English. In American English, people often use the Past Simple with just, already, yet: Did you eat yet?

Hi everyone! It's been a while since my last post, so here's a quick update.

Six months ago, I moved to Lisbon for a new job. The first few weeks were hard: I didn't know anyone and I got lost almost every day. But things have changed a lot since then.

I've been learning Portuguese since I arrived, and I've already made real progress — last week I ordered dinner without switching to English! I haven't become fluent yet, of course, but I still haven't given up.

I've also taken up surfing. I've been going to the beach every Saturday lately, so I'm very tanned (and totally exhausted!). I've fallen off the board more times than I can count, but I've just managed to stand up for ten whole seconds!

Have I made any friends? Yes! I've met some amazing people at work, and we've been planning a trip to Porto for weeks. We haven't booked the tickets yet, but we will soon.

Anyway, I've settled in, and I love it here. Have you ever moved to another country? Tell me in the comments! 💬
